# Angeles National local rules (from the club card)

- No entry into habitat: play as a lateral (red) penalty area. In the build: habitat = red-staked penalty area, not OB.
- Wood chip areas: free drop, no nearer the hole (ground under repair / free relief in the sim).
- Drop areas: holes 3, 6, 9 and 18 have a drop area on the far side of the arroyo (place a drop zone past each arroyo crossing).
